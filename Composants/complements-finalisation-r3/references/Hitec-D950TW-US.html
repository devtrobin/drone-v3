The titanium D950TW is part of our D-Series servo line. All Hitec D-Series servos have been designed using our brand new, cutting edge, custom engineering. Combining industry leading high-resolution, state-of-the-art ultra-response technology with a 32-bit MCU and 12-bit ADC for unprecedented programmability and flexibility, these servos deliver significantly smoother movement. The speedy, powerful line operates on a wide 4.8 ~ 8.4 voltage range, allowing use with any common battery chemistry without the need for adapters or regulators. Featuring Hitec’s industry standard 25-tooth output spline with 3mm hex screw, each D-Series servo brings monster performance never known before in the market.

Features

  • Programmable PWM Digital Amplifier
  • 32-Bit MCU with 12-Bit ADC Circuitry
  • 4096 Resolution
  • 1M Cycle Long Life Potentiometer
  • Wide Operating Voltage (4.8 ~ 8.4 Volts DC)
  • Dual Ball Bearing Supported Output Shaft
  • 25-Tooth Output Spline
  • Coreless Metal Brush Motors
  • Nylon Reinforced Plastic Upper and Lower Case
  • Diamond Cut Aluminum Heat Sink Center Case
  • Dust Resistant and Splash Proof Case

Programmable Functions

  • End Point Adjustments
  • Direction
  • Fail Safe
  • Dead Band
  • Speed (Slower)
  • Soft Start Rate
  • Overload Protection
  • Data Save / Load
  • Program Reset
