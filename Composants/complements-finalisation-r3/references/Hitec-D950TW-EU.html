Precision meets power: This HiTEC servo motor sets the benchmark when accurate positioning and fast response are required. Its coreless motor delivers lively dynamics, while the contact analog potentiometer provides immediate, fine-resolution feedback. As a programmable digital servo with a 32-bit architecture, the control behavior can be tailored precisely to the application. With 343 N·cm (485.7 oz-in) of torque, it offers confident actuation for demanding tasks. Via PWM and TTL, the actuator integrates seamlessly into existing controllers. The IP54 rating secures operation in harsher environments against dust and splashing water. The D950TW combines precise sensing, programmable intelligence, and robust interfaces into a high-performance solution for industrial applications.
