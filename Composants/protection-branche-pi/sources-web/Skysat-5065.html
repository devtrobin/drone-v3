Detailed description

The BLUE SEA 5065-BSS waterproof inline ATO/ATC fuse holder is designed for individual protection of marine electrical circuits. It integrates directly into the wiring, providing a compact solution to add fuse protection where a larger fuse block is unnecessary or impractical.

Waterproof Protection for Marine Environments

This model stands out for its waterproof design, essential for resisting water spray and humidity typical of nautical environments. This feature ensures the reliability of the connection and fuse, reducing risks of corrosion and premature failure compared to non-waterproof fuse holders. It complements installations where main distribution fuse blocks such as BLUE SEA’s ST Blade series are used, by providing localized protection for specific equipment.

Compatibility and Installation

Compatible with ATO and ATC fuse types, this universal inline fuse holder offers flexibility in selecting the appropriate amperage for each circuit. Its inline format simplifies installation, requiring only insertion on the wire of the circuit to be protected. It serves as an alternative or complement to more complex power distribution systems, such as those offered by brands like Garmin or Raymarine, which often integrate protection in their distribution boxes but do not always cover the need for remote individual protection. The 5065-BSS is a fundamental component for electrical safety aboard, preventing overcurrents that could damage equipment or cause incidents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Need help? Contact us

What is the purpose of the Blue Sea 5065-BSS waterproof inline ATO/ATC fuse holder?

The Blue Sea 5065-BSS inline ATO/ATC waterproof fuse holder is designed to provide individual and localized protection to marine electrical circuits. It is installed directly on the wiring to protect a specific piece of equipment from overcurrents where a larger fuse block would be impractical.

Which fuse types are compatible with this fuse holder?

This fuse holder is compatible with ATO (Automotive Type Original) and ATC (Automotive Type Closed) fuses, providing flexibility in selecting the appropriate amperage for each circuit.

What is the main feature of this fuse holder?

The main feature of this fuse holder is its watertight design. It is specifically designed to withstand water spray and humidity, which is essential to ensure the reliability and durability of electrical connections in marine environments.

How do you install this fuse holder on an electrical circuit?

Installation is straightforward: the fuse holder is integrated directly inline on the wire of the circuit you wish to protect. You need to cut the wire, strip the ends, then connect the fuse holder using suitable connectors (not included) to ensure a secure and watertight connection.

Is this fuse holder suitable for marine environments?

Yes, absolutely. Its watertight design and rugged construction are specifically suited to withstand the demanding conditions of marine environments, including water spray, humidity, and corrosion.

What is the maximum voltage supported by the fuse holder 5065-BSS?

The Blue Sea 5065-BSS fuse holder can support a maximum voltage of 32 volts DC.

What is the maximum amperage that the 5065-BSS fuse holder can support?

This fuse holder is designed to support a maximum current rating of 30 amperes. It is important to select a fuse with a current rating suitable for the circuit to be protected, without exceeding this limit for the fuse holder itself.

What is the recommended cable size for this fuse holder?

The 5065-BSS fuse block is equipped with pre-installed 12 AWG (American Wire Gauge) wires, which corresponds to the recommended cable size for optimal connection.

Is this fuse holder supplied with fuses included?

No, the Blue Sea 5065-BSS fuse holder is sold separately. The ATO/ATC fuses of the desired amperage must be purchased separately to match the specific requirements of your circuit.

Can this fuse holder be used for automotive or other applications?

Yes, although designed for marine environments, this fuse holder can be used in other applications such as automotive or recreational vehicles, provided the voltage (32V DC) and amperage (30A max) specifications match the requirements of the circuit to be protected, and waterproofing is a desired feature.

What are the advantages of a waterproof inline fuse holder compared to a fuse block?

An inline waterproof fuse holder offers several advantages:

Localized protection: It allows protecting a single device or a specific circuit without requiring a larger fuse block.Space saving: Its compact size is ideal for tight spaces.Superior waterproofing: Its waterproof design protects the fuse and connection from moisture and corrosion, which is crucial in exposed environments.Installation flexibility: It can be installed anywhere along the circuit wire.

How to ensure you select the correct fuse amperage for this fuse holder?

To select the correct fuse rating, you need to know the rated current of the device or circuit you wish to protect. The fuse must be slightly higher than the normal operating current of the device, but low enough to melt and interrupt the circuit in the event of an overcurrent, thereby protecting the equipment and wiring.

Are specific tools required to install the 5065-BSS fuse holder?

Installation requires basic electrical wiring tools, such as wire strippers to prepare the wires, crimping pliers to secure connectors (if using terminals or heat-shrink tubing), and optionally a voltage tester to check the circuit.

Does this fuse holder provide short-circuit protection?

Yes, the primary function of a fuse is to protect the circuit against overcurrents, including those caused by a short circuit. In the event of a short circuit, the fuse will melt quickly, interrupting the current flow and thus protecting the wiring and connected equipment from potential damage.

Is the 5065-BSS fuse holder resistant to marine corrosion?

Yes, thanks to its high-quality materials, such as a durable plastic body and tinned copper contacts, as well as its waterproof design (IP66), the 5065-BSS fuse holder is designed to provide excellent resistance to corrosion, even in salty and humid marine environments.

Where is it recommended to install this type of fuse holder in a circuit?

The fuse holder should generally be installed as close as possible to the power source or the device it is intended to protect. This ensures that most of the wiring is protected against overcurrent.

What is the brand of this fuse holder?

This fuse holder is manufactured by Blue Sea Systems, a brand known for its high-quality electrical products for marine applications and specialized vehicles.
