QuicRun WP 16BL30G2
Intelligent Freewheeling
for Lower Heat
and Enhanced Performance
  • The ESC features intelligent freewheeling technology,
  • delivering higher drive efficiency, lower operating
  • temperatures, improved stability, and greater reliability.
Compact Size for Easier
Installation and Flexible Layout
  • Compared to the previous generation 16BL30 ESC, the G2 ESC is significantly smaller:
  • 8.3% shorter in length, 7.1% narrower, and 16.7% lower in height. This design is compact
  • and makes installation easier and allows for cleaner wiring on the chassis.
BEC Upgrade

Compared to the G1 ESC,

The BEC’s continuous current
has been upgraded from
1A to 2A.
A peak current
of up to
4A
The output voltage can
be switched between
6V and 7.4V.
Easily powering high-torque and high-voltage servos. This provides
faster response and more precise control.
High Protection Rating
  • IP67-rated protection allows it to easily handle harsh environments such as rain, sand,
  • and muddy roads, ensuring reliable performance without environmental limitations.
Note: The IP67 protection rating described above applies under normal usage conditions, providing splash resistance, water resistance, and
dust resistance, as tested under controlled laboratory conditions. These protective features are not permanent, and protection performance
may degrade over time due to regular wear and aging. Do not submerge the product in water for extended periods, as this may cause damage.
Multiple Programming Methods
  • Supports three programming methods: LCD Program Box Pro, LED Program Box,
  • and SET button, allowing users to conveniently select their preferred
  • programming method for parameter adjustments.

Perfect Match for
QuicRun 2030 G2/2435 G3 Motors

  • QuicRun 2030 G2/2435 G3 motors, delivering smoother control.
Wide Compatibility
  • The power system is suitable for both
    1/16th & 1/18th scale hobby vehicles.
